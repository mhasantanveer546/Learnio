Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect the minimum data needed to provide Learnio's services:

  • Account data: username, email, password (hashed)
  • Study materials: files you upload (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, TXT, images)
  • Generated content: AI summaries, quizzes, flashcards, chat messages
  • Usage data: quiz scores, flashcard progress, last login time

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the study tools you request (quizzes, summaries, flashcards)
  • To power the AI chat using your uploaded material as context
  • To track your learning progress
  • To send important service notifications (rarely)

We do NOT: sell your data, use it for advertising, or train AI models on it.

3. Third-Party Services

We use these services to operate Learnio:

  • Google Gemini: AI generation (your material is sent to Google's API)
  • Backblaze R2: File storage
  • Neon: Database hosting
  • Vercel: Application hosting
  • Sentry: Error monitoring (no personal data, just error traces)

4. Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. When you delete a study material, we delete the file from storage and remove its data from our database. If you delete your account, all your data is permanently removed within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data

Contact us at mhasantanveer546@gmail.com to exercise these rights.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Files are stored with signed URLs that expire after 5 minutes. We use CSRF protection and rate limiting to prevent abuse.
